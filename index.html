Foundation
What is AI GRC?
Managing AI through structured governance, rigorous risk management, and regulatory compliance.
G

Governance

Policies, roles, accountability, and oversight mechanisms guiding AI design, deployment, and monitoring.

R

Risk

Identifying and mitigating AI harms — bias, hallucination, data poisoning, model drift, supply chain risks.

C

Compliance

Meeting EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, and sector-specific regulatory mandates.

The Stakes
Why AI GRC Matters Now
Regulatory action is no longer theoretical.
Enforcement

Regulatory Enforcement is Live

EU AI Act phased enforcement began 2024. Fines reach €35M or 7% of global turnover — whichever is higher. Article 50 transparency obligations now mandatory.

Finance

Financial Scrutiny

FCA and PRA supervisory guidance on AI model risk is active.

Privacy

Data & Privacy

GDPR, training data rights, and Article 22 create complex obligations.

Global

Fragmentation

US, EU, UK, China, Singapore — diverging philosophies need unified strategy.

Board

Board-Level Accountability & Third-Party Risk

Directors are personally accountable for AI risks. Vendor AI models inherit regulatory obligations across your supply chain. Governance must flow from board to engineering.
